Request removal

  1. Email richardcen05@gmail.com with the subject Research data deletion request.
  2. Include the public Threads or YouTube URL, or the platform content identifier, that you want removed. Do not send a password, API key, identity document, or other secret.
  3. The researcher will confirm whether the record exists, remove it from active raw and analytical files, and exclude it from the next data refresh.

Requests are normally completed within 14 days. If the content has already been aggregated into a non-identifying result, the underlying record will still be removed, but an aggregate statistic that cannot be linked back to the requester may remain.

Automatic platform-data lifecycle

YouTube titles, creator names, descriptions, comments, and related source metadata are refreshed or deleted within 30 days. Deleted or unavailable platform content is removed when detected during refresh. The project does not retain API access tokens in the research dataset.

Academy Sentiment Research Pilot

A non-commercial undergraduate research prototype that studies public discussion about Apple Developer Academy in Indonesia.

Privacy Policy Terms of Service Methodology Data Deletion
This is a research information site, not a public analytics product. It has no user accounts, advertising, payment, or content publishing features.

Research purpose

The project evaluates TF-IDF with Linear SVM and IndoBERTweet for three-class sentiment classification using public YouTube comments about Apple Developer Academy in Indonesia. It reports aggregate findings rather than profiles of individual users.

Uses YouTube Data API Services

The pilot discovers relevant public videos and retrieves public comments and replies for research feasibility. Sentiment labels and model outputs are independently calculated research metrics, not metrics supplied or approved by YouTube. They will only be created after the required Analytics & Reporting use case is accepted.

Outputs

Research outputs may include aggregate class distributions, model evaluation metrics, and paraphrased examples. Raw comments, account names, user IDs, and access credentials are not published.

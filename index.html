Independent academic research

Academy Sentiment Research Pilot

A non-commercial undergraduate research prototype that studies public discussion about Apple Developer Academy in Indonesia.

Privacy Policy Terms of Service Data Deletion
This is a research information site, not a public analytics product. It has no user accounts, advertising, payment, or content publishing features.

Research purpose

The project evaluates TF-IDF with Linear SVM and IndoBERTweet for three-class sentiment classification. It uses only public content returned by official platform APIs, keeps platform identity in the analysis, and reports aggregate findings rather than profiles of individual users.

Uses YouTube Data API Services

The pilot discovers relevant videos and retrieves public comments for research feasibility. YouTube-derived sentiment analysis will only begin after the required Analytics & Reporting approval is accepted.

YouTube Terms of Service · Google Privacy Policy

Uses Threads API

The pilot searches public Threads posts by frozen research keywords. Replies are excluded unless the separate permission required to read them has been approved.

Threads Supplemental Privacy Policy

Outputs

Research outputs may include aggregate class distributions, model evaluation metrics, and paraphrased examples. Raw public posts, comments, account names, user IDs, and access credentials are not published.

Research Methodology

A research-only workflow for public YouTube comments about Apple Developer Academy in Indonesia.

No sentiment labels, predictions, or model results have been produced. Those steps begin only after the Analytics & Reporting use case is accepted.

Research workflow

  1. Discovery: 18 frozen query families identify public videos using search.list and videos.list.
  2. Video audit: candidates are manually checked for relevance and source type before any comment collection.
  3. Comment collection: public comments and replies are retrieved with commentThreads.list and comments.list, including pagination.
  4. Relevance screening: text is retained only when it discusses Apple Developer Academy in Indonesia.
  5. Sentiment experiment: after approval, human labels support a controlled comparison of TF-IDF–Linear SVM and IndoBERTweet.

Pilot evidence

  • 231 video candidates inspected; 107 judged relevant from public metadata.
  • 30 videos selected: 3 official, 6 partner, and 21 public sources.
  • 858 unique comments and replies within the study period, from 23 videos that returned data.
  • 139 of 190 audited texts were relevant. This is a feasibility check, not a population estimate or sentiment result.

Data protection and reproducibility

Usernames and channel identifiers are excluded from the analytical dataset. Raw YouTube API data is refreshed or deleted within 30 days. Raw comments are not published. Query families, selection rules, code versions, pagination state, and aggregate counts are documented so the research process can be audited without exposing personal identifiers.
